Please add a luxury AI Stylist / Personal Shopping Assistant widget to the platform that helps customers choose sizes, select colors, pair outfits, and answer questions about pre-owned formalwear.

Here are the specific layout, styling, and functional requirements for the AI Assistant:

---

### 1. Floating AI Assistant Launcher & Chat Interface
- **Floating Button / Trigger**:
  - Add a sleek, sticky chat button at the bottom-right corner (or bottom-left in Arabic RTL mode) with a luxury sparkle/stylist icon.
  - Label badge: "المستشارة الشخصية | AI Stylist"
- **Chat Window Aesthetic**:
  - A clean, modern slide-up panel or modal following the neutral luxury palette (soft off-white/cream background, charcoal text, soft rose gold accents).
  - Header: Displays assistant name (e.g., "نورا - مستشارتكِ الخاصة" / "Noura - Your Personal AI Stylist") with an "Online" status indicator and a language toggle option matching the site's default language (Arabic / English).

---

### 2. Core Capabilities & Interactive Features
- **Size & Fit Guide Assistant**:
  - Asks the customer for their body measurements (bust, waist, hips, height) or standard US/UK/EU dress sizes.
  - Recommends the exact size to select for specific dresses/suits listed on the site and warns if a vintage or tailored item runs small/large.
- **Color & Occasion Advisor**:
  - Suggests dress colors based on skin undertones, event themes (e.g., Iraqi weddings, henna nights, formal galas, graduation ceremonies), and season.
- **Mix & Match / Accessories Recommendation**:
  - Suggests matching shoes, bags, or jewelry from the store catalog to complete an outfit.
- **Pre-owned Quality & Authenticity Q&A**:
  - Answers questions regarding item condition tags ("Like New", "Worn Once"), dry-cleaning protocols, and the return/exchange policy.

---

### 4. Smart Integration with Shop Catalog
- The AI assistant should have full context of the active products in the shop catalog.
- When recommending an item, it must display interactive **Product Cards directly inside the chat window** showing the dress photo, title, size, and price in IQD (د.ع) with a direct "View Dress" or "Add to Cart" button.

### 5. Full Bilingual & Directional Support
- The AI chat interface must seamlessly adapt to Arabic (RTL) when the main site is set to Arabic, and English (LTR) when switched to English.
